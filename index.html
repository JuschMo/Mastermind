How to Play

🧩 How to Play

  • The teacher enters a list of sentences or single words into the word box at the top – a minimum of 4, ideally around 9.
  • The teacher secretly chooses a combination of 4 words or phrases and notes them on a piece of paper.
  • Students guess a combination by saying the word or phrase in the target language. For extra challenge, students can translate the words, put them into sentences, or create a short narration using them.
  • Words can be added to the attempt boxes by dragging, double-clicking, or selecting from a drop-down menu in the attempt box.
  • After each attempt, the teacher gives feedback by changing the symbols next to each guessed word:
    • 🟩 Green = word is correct and in the right place
    • 🟨 Yellow = word is in the sequence but in the wrong place
    • 🟥 Red = word is not in the sequence
  • The row is then locked.
  • Students use the clues to improve the next guess and try to find the correct sequence in as few attempts as possible!
